### Menuyukti – The Social Menu

Menuyukti transforms the traditional digital menu into a **living, social experience**.

Instead of simply showing dishes, the menu shows what is happening around them: **what’s trending, what guests recommend, real customer photos, new dishes, limited availability, and what’s popular right now.**

Guests can interact directly with dishes by recommending them, saving them, sharing photos, or discovering what others love — without turning the experience into another review platform.

Combined with Menuyukti’s AI and inventory data, the menu can also adapt dynamically, highlighting relevant dishes, hiding unavailable items, and helping guests decide **what to eat**.

The result is a menu that doesn’t just display food — **it helps people discover it.**
