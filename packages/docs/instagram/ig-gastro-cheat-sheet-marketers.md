# Instagram for Restaurants — Marketer's Guide

A plain-language cheat sheet for restaurant teams. No flowcharts — just what to post, where, and why.

**More detail:** [full cheat sheet](./ig-gastro-cheat-sheet.md) · **deep playbook:** [instagram-for-gastro.md](./instagram-for-gastro.md)

---

## The big picture

Think of your Instagram in four layers:

| Layer          | Plain English               | Job                                              |
| -------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Profile**    | Your front door             | Tell strangers who you are and what to do next   |
| **Reels**      | Your window display         | Catch the eye of people who don't follow you yet |
| **Posts**      | Your menu board on the wall | Build trust — dishes, proof, story               |
| **Stories**    | A quick chat with regulars  | Stay human, stay top of mind, share today's news |
| **Highlights** | FAQ folders under your name | Answers guests look up before they visit         |

---

## Your profile — dress the front door

When someone lands on your profile, they ask three things in seconds: _What is this place? Would I like it? How do I go?_

### What it should look like (checklist)

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Photo]  Luna Trattoria · Mitte        │
│           @lunatrattoria                │
│                                         │
│  Neapolitan pizza · Kreuzberg           │
│  Wood-fired since 2019 · vegan options  │
│  Book a table ↓                         │
│  🔗 Reserve · Menu · Order              │
│                                         │
│  [ Call ]  [ Email ]  [ Directions ]    │
│                                         │
│  📌 Pin 1: Signature dish               │
│  📌 Pin 2: Reviews / guest photos       │
│  📌 Pin 3: How to find us & book        │
│                                         │
│  Highlights: Menu | Book | Location |   │
│              Reviews | Events | FAQ     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Username + bio — keep it simple

**Username**

- Same name as on your door, Google, and reservation system
- Easy to spell after someone recommends you at dinner

**Bio — four short lines**

1. What you serve (cuisine or vibe)
2. Where you are (neighborhood or city)
3. One reason to trust you (signature dish, years open, award)
4. What to do next (Book · Order · Walk in · DM for events)

**Link**

- One link with 1–3 actions only — don't send people to a maze

**Pins**

- Pin your best dish, your best proof, and how to visit

## Which format should I use?

Don't start with "we filmed something." Start with **what you want the guest to feel or do.**

### Pick Reels when you want…

**New people to discover you.**

Reels are how Instagram shows your restaurant to locals and visitors who have never heard of you. Think sizzle, steam, pouring, plating, the room buzzing, the team in action.

| Great for                     | Skip when                       |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Mouth-watering food in motion | Full menu with prices           |
| Kitchen & team personality    | Offers with lots of fine print  |
| Atmosphere & vibe             | Updates only your regulars need |
| One clear idea in ~15 seconds |                                 |

**Tips:** Film vertical (phone upright). Hook in the first 2 seconds. Add text on screen — many people watch muted.

**Rough rhythm:** 1–2 Reels per week (small team) · 3–5 (larger team)

---

### Pick Stories when you want…

**A personal connection with people who already follow you.**

Stories are the friendly, informal layer. They don't need to be perfect. A "happy weekend," "good luck this week," thank-you after a busy Friday, or a quick wave from the team often lands better than another sales slide.

| Great for                                 | Skip when                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Weekend / week greetings                  | Info guests need months later |
| Thank-yous & team moments                 | Long stories better as a post |
| Today's special & sold-out notes          |                               |
| Happy hour, lunch deal, event tonight     |                               |
| Polls: "Which dish should we bring back?" |                               |
| Link to book or order _when it matters_   |                               |

**Mix relational and useful.** Not every Story needs a "Book now" sticker. Connection first; action when there's something to act on.

**Rough rhythm:** A few frames most days you're open (2–4 is enough)

**Story types at a glance**

| Type      | Example                                          |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Hello** | "Happy weekend from the terrace team"            |
| **Today** | "Soup of the day: pumpkin · market fish limited" |
| **Ask**   | Poll on dessert or wine pairing                  |
| **Act**   | Link sticker for reservations or tickets         |

---

### Pick Posts when you want…

**Something on your grid that builds trust over time.**

New guests scroll your feed like they'd scan photos on the wall. Posts stay. They answer: _Is the food real? Is this place my kind of place?_

**Single photo** — one hero dish, room shot, award, hiring post

**Carousel (swipe post)** — best when people need to read or compare:

- Menus and new seasonal items
- "5 dishes to try on your first visit"
- Step-by-step: how a plate comes together
- Reviews, press quotes, guest photo sets
- Allergens, pairings, event details

**Caption recipe:** Hook → a bit of context → proof → what to do next

_Example:_ "Lunch in 12 minutes on weekdays · Popular with nearby offices · Our top 3 reorders this month · Reserve or DM for groups."

**Rough rhythm:** 3–4 posts per week (small team) · 5–7 (larger team)

---

### Pick Highlights when you want…

**Evergreen answers sitting under your profile name.**

Highlights are not a junk drawer for old Stories. They're the folders a first-time guest taps before visiting.

| Highlight            | Put this inside                                   |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| **Menu**             | Signatures, categories, dietary notes (V, VG, GF) |
| **Reserve / Order**  | How to book, order links, group info              |
| **Location & hours** | Address, map, parking, holiday hours              |
| **Reviews**          | Guest photos, quotes, press                       |
| **Events**           | Brunch, live music, private dining, catering      |
| **FAQ**              | Kids, dogs, allergens, busy times, payment        |

Use matching cover icons and short titles. Refresh when the menu or hours change.

## Quick reference — one table

| If the guest needs to…          | Use this                         |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Discover you for the first time | **Reel**                         |
| Feel connected to you today     | **Story**                        |
| Act today (offer, book tonight) | **Story** (with link)            |
| Save ideas for a future visit   | **Carousel post**                |
| Trust you from your grid        | **Post** or **carousel**         |
| Look up hours, menu, FAQ later  | **Highlight** (+ pin on profile) |

## What to post — healthy mix

A simple starting balance (adjust after a month of watching saves, DMs, and bookings):

```
Trust & story     ████████████████  40%   team, chef, reviews, values
Food & vibe       ████████████████  40%   dishes, kitchen, atmosphere
Offers & events   ████████          20%   promos, bookings, launches
```

If everything is a discount, guests wait for discounts. Keep most content appetite and trust; use offers with a clear reason and time window.

**Good offer:** "Weekday lunch Mon–Fri 11:00–14:00 — 10% off mains for nearby offices."

**Weak offer:** "10% OFF!!!" with no day, time, or item.

## A realistic week (small team)

| When              | What                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Most days**     | 2–4 Stories — mix a hello, something from today, and a reshare or link when relevant |
| **2× per week**   | Reel — food in motion, BTS, or atmosphere                                            |
| **3–4× per week** | Feed post or carousel — dish, proof, or menu moment                                  |
| **Ongoing**       | Reply to DMs and comments the same day when you can                                  |
| **Monthly**       | Check Highlights and pinned posts — still accurate?                                  |

**Monday** — Story: "Good start to the week" + today's lunch  
**Wednesday** — Reel: plating or sizzle shot · Carousel: one signature dish explained  
**Friday** — Story: "Happy weekend" + walk-in or event note  
**Sunday** — Light Story or rest; plan next week's 2–3 hero shots

(This is a pattern, not a rigid calendar — adapt to your service days.)

---

## Signs it's working

**Weekly (on Instagram)**  
Reach · saves · shares · profile visits · new DMs

**Monthly (in the restaurant)**  
More reservations, walk-ins, orders, or repeat guests — and staff hearing "I saw you on Instagram"

Ask at the host stand: _How did you hear about us?_

---

## Three habits that matter most

1. **Profile ready** — bio, link, pins, and Highlights answer the basics
2. **Reels for strangers, Stories for regulars, Posts for proof**
3. **Sound like a person** — especially in Stories; that's where loyalty grows
