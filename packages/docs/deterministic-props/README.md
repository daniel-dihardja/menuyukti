# ⭐ MATRIX DISTRIBUTION (Structural Economics)

Defines the financial architecture of the menu.

---

## Star Profit Share

**Definition:** Percentage of total profit generated by star items.  
**Why it matters:** High values signal dependency risk.

---

## Low-End Menu Share

**Definition:** Portion of menu occupied by weak performers.  
**Why it matters:** Menu bloat reduces customer clarity and operational efficiency.

---

## Puzzle Profit Contribution

**Definition:** Margin share from high-margin but under-ordered items.  
**Why it matters:** Represents hidden profit capacity marketing can unlock.

---

## Profit Concentration Index

**Definition:** Degree to which profit relies on a small subset of items.  
**Why it matters:** High concentration increases financial fragility.

---

## Menu Diversification Score

**Definition:** Evenness of profit distribution.  
**Why it matters:** Balanced portfolios absorb shocks better.

---

## Structural Health Score

**Definition:** Composite snapshot of menu economic stability.  
**Why it matters:** Provides executives a fast resilience check.

---

# ⭐ MATRIX ITEMS (Product-Level Economics)

Identifies where money is actually created.

---

## Profit Velocity

**Definition:** `margin_per_unit × quantity`  
**Why it matters:** Measures how FAST profit accumulates. Fast-profit items deserve attention first.

---

## Contribution Margin Share

**Definition:** Percentage of total profit generated by the item.  
**Why it matters:** Items above ~10% often stabilize the business.

---

## Margin Strength Index

**Definition:** Margin normalized relative to the menu median.  
**Why it matters:** Enables intelligent comparisons across restaurants.

---

## Volume Strength Index

**Definition:** Adoption relative to the menu median.  
**Why it matters:** Customers already validated the product.

---

## Price Power Indicator

**Definition:** Strong margin + strong volume.  
**Why it matters:** Signals tolerance for small price increases — the highest ROI lever in hospitality.

---

## Growth Lever Score

**Definition:** High margin + low adoption.  
**Why it matters:** Represents sleeping profit that promotion can unlock quickly.

---

## Menu Slot Efficiency

**Definition:** Profit generated relative to menu space consumed.  
**Why it matters:** Menus are finite cognitive real estate.

---

## Drag Indicator

**Definition:** Low margin AND low volume.  
**Why it matters:** These items dilute operational focus and should rarely be promoted.

---

## Category Profit Dominance

**Definition:** Profit share captured by a category.  
**Why it matters:** Reveals the restaurant’s true economic identity.

---

# ⭐ HEATMAPS (Behavioral Demand Intelligence)

Explains WHEN revenue is influenceable.

---

## Total Demand

**Definition:** Aggregate item sales.  
**Why it matters:** High-demand products create leverage.

---

## Peak Hour

**Definition:** Hour with maximum sales.  
**Why it matters:** Marketing should occur BEFORE the peak.

---

## Peak Demand Share

**Definition:** Percentage of sales within the peak hour.  
**Why it matters:** High concentration allows precision marketing.

---

## Top-3 Hour Concentration

**Definition:** Share of demand captured by the strongest hours.  
**Why it matters:** Narrow windows are easier to influence.

---

## Weekday Demand Share

**Definition:** Portion of demand occurring Monday–Friday.  
**Why it matters:** Indicates routine behavior — the safest revenue type.

---

## Routine Strength Score

**Definition:** Stability of recurring purchase patterns.  
**Why it matters:** Routine customers generate predictable cashflow.

---

## Dead Hour Count

**Definition:** Hours with negligible demand.  
**Why it matters:** Represents revenue creation opportunities.

---

## Demand Predictability

**Definition:** Variability across time periods.  
**Why it matters:** Predictable demand unlocks automation.

---

## Momentum Indicator

**Definition:** Speed of demand acceleration toward peaks.  
**Why it matters:** Capturing customers BEFORE the surge maximizes ROI.

---

## Daypart Ownership

**Definition:** Identification of time segments dominated by the item.  
**Why it matters:** Supports strategic positioning (e.g., breakfast anchor).

# 🔥 NEW — PROMOTION DECISION PRIMITIVES (CRITICAL FOR INSTAGRAM AGENT)

These properties convert economic + behavioral intelligence into **promotion-ready decisions.**

Without them, agents become content generators instead of revenue allocators.

---

## Promotion Eligibility Flag

**Definition:** Binary filter determining whether an item is allowed to be promoted.

**Example Logic:**

- Margin strength above threshold
- Not a drag item
- Not operationally constrained (future)

**Why it matters:**  
Agents should filter BEFORE scoring.  
Great systems eliminate bad options early.

---

## Promotion Leverage Score

**Definition:** Estimated revenue lift potential if the item receives additional attention.

**Derived From:**

- margin strength
- growth lever score
- current volume
- demand headroom

**Why it matters:**  
Not all items benefit equally from promotion.  
This predicts economic upside.

---

## Demand Headroom

**Definition:** Remaining capacity for demand growth before operational strain.

**Signals:**

- moderate vs extreme volume
- kitchen throughput (future)
- demand saturation

**Why it matters:**  
Promoting already-maxed items creates queues and degrades experience.

Elite agents know when **NOT** to promote.

---

## Visual Marketability Heuristic

**Definition:** Likelihood an item performs well on visual platforms.

**Simple Early Proxy:**

- drinks
- desserts
- colorful dishes
- layered foods

**Why it matters:**  
Instagram is attention-driven.  
Some products naturally convert impressions into visits.

This is not vanity — it affects revenue efficiency.

---

## Promotion Fatigue Guardrail

**Definition:** Prevents repeated promotion of the same item within a defined window.

**Example Rule:**
`not promoted in last 14–30 days`

**Why it matters:**  
Variety protects engagement and brand freshness.

---

## Promotion Timing Confidence

**Definition:** Confidence level derived from demand concentration and predictability.

**Why it matters:**  
Agents should be more aggressive when behavioral certainty is high.

Predictable demand → safer promotion.

---

## Cross-Sell Potential (Optional but High Value)

**Definition:** Likelihood that promoting this item increases basket size.

**Derived From Later:**

- bundle history
- category pairing
- attach rates

**Why it matters:**  
The best promotions grow **ticket size**, not just traffic.
