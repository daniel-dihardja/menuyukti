# Menuyukti Concept

Menuyukti is a SaaS for restaurant marketers that turns POS sales exports into actionable social‑media marketing.

## Scope (Phase 1)

**Primary output: Instagram posts.**

Menuyukti produces:
- what to post (which menu items deserve attention)
- when to post (timing based on demand heatmaps)
- why it should work (clear, deterministic reasoning)

This is the lowest‑risk, highest‑leverage marketing channel for restaurants and provides a tight feedback loop.

## Phase 2 (Expansion)

**Promos and combos (suggested ideas only).**

These are part of restaurant marketing, but they are more operationally risky and margin‑sensitive.  
We’ll introduce them as optional recommendations after Instagram outputs are reliable.

## Why This Ordering

Instagram recommendations are easier to validate, require less coordination, and map directly to the analytics + action pipeline.

Promos/combos come later because they require:
- pricing discipline
- margin protection
- operational readiness (inventory, staffing, prep)

## Product Summary (One Sentence)

Menuyukti turns POS sales data into Instagram‑ready marketing—what to post, when to post, and why it will work—then expands into promo and combo ideas once the foundation is proven.
