How I would approach your goal

Project goal (your words): AI-driven marketing automation across all paid ads
platforms and other relevant channels, ending with a fully functional system.

I would implement this in three phases so you see value early while hardening
integrations and governance.

Phase 1 — Foundation (weeks 1–2)
  - Discovery: lounge positioning, priority nights/events, geo radius, offers,
    brand voice, existing ad accounts (Meta Business, Google Ads, etc.), and
    current organic channels (Instagram, email, WhatsApp, website, listings).
  - Automation architecture: central “campaign record” (brief → assets → variants
    → schedule → channel payloads), same pattern as Menuyukti milestones.
  - Connect first paid + organic pair (typically Meta Ads + Instagram/Facebook
    organic, or Meta + Google Local/Performance Max if search intent matters).
  - Human-in-the-loop: nothing publishes or spends without explicit approval.

Phase 2 — Multi-channel expansion (weeks 3–5)
  - Add remaining paid platforms you actively use (e.g. Google Ads, TikTok Ads
    if relevant to your demographic, retargeting where you have pixel/data).
  - Add secondary channels: email promos, story/reel cadence, event announcements,
    optional creative variants for seasonal menus or guest DJs/themes.
  - Reporting loop: pull performance metrics back into the system so the next
    brief generation is informed by what worked (creative, audience, day-part).

Phase 3 — Operational “fully functional” state (weeks 6+)
  - Playbooks per use case: “slow Tuesday,” “weekend vibe,” “private event push,”
    “new cocktail/menu launch,” with templates and guardrails.
  - Evaluation rules on generated copy and creatives (brand tone, compliance,
    prohibited claims, local language).
  - Runbooks and handover: how your team approves, edits, and reruns automations
    without needing me for daily operations.

What “fully functional” means in practice

  - Paid: automated draft campaigns/ad sets/creatives and copy variants generated
    from your briefs and performance data; API-ready export or direct publish
    where accounts and policies allow — always behind approval for spend.
  - Organic & other: scheduled content packages (posts, stories, reels scripts),
    email drafts, and reusable asset prompts aligned to lounge positioning.
  - One place to see what was generated, what passed QA, what is approved, and
    what went live — not scattered chats.

Relevant technical scope (transparent)

  I build the automation product layer: orchestration, integrations, UX for
  reviewers, analytics-informed generation, and quality checks. For ad platforms
  I integrate via official APIs (Meta Marketing API, Google Ads API, etc.) using
  your business accounts. If you already work with a media buyer or agency, I
  design the system to complement them — automation drafts, humans approve spend.

  If any platform is not in active use, I will not force it; “all relevant channels”
  should mean the channels that actually drive bookings and footfall for your lounge.

What I need from you to start

  - Access or admin contact for ad accounts and organic profiles
  - Brand guidelines, example posts/ads you like, and off-limits messaging
  - Priority outcomes for the next 90 days (e.g. event nights, brunch, bottle service,
