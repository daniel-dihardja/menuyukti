# Weak items review

Weak items are low-popularity and low-margin dishes. They are candidates to **reprice,
improve, or remove** — not default promotion heroes.

## How to use it

- Load `weak_review` when the operator asks about weak items, cuts, or retiring dishes.
- Ground advice in reprice / improve / remove from the loaded summary.
- Do not pivot a weak-items question into a full promo calendar or channel plan.

## Caveats

- A day specialty can be weak in BCG terms but still worth a limited feature.
- Always use chart data for which items qualify; do not invent margins.
