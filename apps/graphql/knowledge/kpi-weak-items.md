# Weak items review

Weak items (Low End / BCG “dogs”) are low-popularity and low-margin dishes.
They are candidates to **reprice, improve, or remove** — not default Instagram
heroes.

## How to use it

- Load `low_end_review` when the operator asks about weak items, cuts, or dogs.
- Ground advice in reprice / improve / remove from the loaded summary.
- Do not pivot a weak-items question into a full weekly Instagram schedule.

## Caveats

- A day specialty can be low_end in BCG terms but still worth a limited feature.
- Always use chart data for which items qualify; do not invent margins.
